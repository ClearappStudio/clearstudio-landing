About this policy

This policy applies to PRISMA by Clear Studio, an iPad personal budgeting application. Clear Studio is an independent project of Francisco Olmedo Ariza, based in Spain. For privacy enquiries, email hello@clearstudio.app. Website browsing is covered separately.

Financial information you enter

PRISMA lets you manage income, expenses, budgets, savings, accounts and categories. This information is entered and managed by you. PRISMA does not require you to connect a bank account to use its current budgeting features. Your entries are not intended to be transmitted to Clear Studio servers.

Local storage and backups

The current release is designed to store your budget records on your device. It does not offer iCloud synchronisation between devices. If you export a backup or document, the destination you choose controls where that copy is stored. Apple's device backup services may separately include app data according to your device settings. Protect exported files as they may contain sensitive financial details.

Smart features

PRISMA may use Apple-provided intelligence features to help organise financial entries. The precise processing route and any network use must be verified against the release build before this policy is published as final. Do not include confidential information in support requests.

Advertising, tracking and services

PRISMA is not designed to show advertisements or track you across other companies' apps or websites. Apple may separately process App Store purchases, downloads and diagnostics under Apple's privacy policy. A final review of included SDKs and network requests is required to confirm whether any other data is collected by the app or its service providers.

If you contact us

If you email Clear Studio, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you voluntarily provide. We use these details to respond to your request, with our email provider processing messages to deliver the service. We keep support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for support and legal obligations. Please avoid sending complete financial records unless specifically necessary.

Your choices and rights

You can edit or delete financial entries inside PRISMA. Removing the app may remove its local records, subject to device backups and copies you exported elsewhere. To remove those additional copies, manage them separately where they are stored. For personal data you send directly to Clear Studio, contact us to request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability where applicable, or to object to processing. You can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

Changes to this policy

We will revise this policy if PRISMA's features or data practices change. Draft prepared: 21 November 2026.
