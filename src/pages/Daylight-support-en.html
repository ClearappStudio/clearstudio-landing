Getting started

Do I need an account or an Apple Pencil?

No Daylight account is required. You can type tasks and notes with the keyboard, or write and draw with Apple Pencil. Daylight is designed for iPad and requires iPadOS 17 or later.

iCloud

How do I sync my devices?

Open Daylight’s Settings and enable iCloud on each device. Use the same Apple Account and turn on iCloud Drive in the device settings. Changes sync automatically when iCloud is available; you can keep working offline.

What does “changes queued for iCloud” mean?

Daylight has prepared your changes for iCloud. Apple manages the transfer, so this message does not confirm that another device has received them. Check your connection and iCloud Drive, then open Daylight on the other device. You can also use Check sync in Settings.

Why am I being asked to review versions?

This can happen when local and iCloud copies have changed independently, or when connecting an existing agenda for the first time. Daylight does not automatically merge conflicting agendas. Export any versions you want to keep, then choose the one to use. Local recovery copies are saved before replacement.

Your data

How do backups and recovery work?

You can export an agenda backup and restore it using Open backup. Restoring replaces your current agenda and can sync that replacement to your other devices when iCloud is enabled. Export your current agenda first. Recovery copies created during sync are separate from the Trash.

Can I recover something I deleted?

Deleted tasks, notes and habits stay in Trash for 30 days unless you delete them permanently sooner. After that period they are removed when Daylight next cleans up the Trash. Exported backups and local recovery copies are separate and are not automatically erased after 30 days.

Calendars

Why can’t I see my calendar events?

Connect Calendar in Daylight’s Settings and choose the calendars to display. If you previously denied access, review the Calendar permission for Daylight in iPad Settings. Events are managed through their calendar account; they are separate from Daylight’s iCloud notes and tasks.

Writing

How do writing and calendar interactions work?

Use PencilKit tools for handwriting. In calendar areas, switch to Annotate to write over the schedule and events, then finish annotating to return to event interaction. You can change the language, week start, palette and other preferences in Settings.
