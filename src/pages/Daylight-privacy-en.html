About this policy

This policy describes the Daylight by Clear Studio iPad app. Clear Studio is the independent project of Francisco Olmedo Ariza, based in Spain. For privacy questions, contact hello@clearstudio.app. This policy does not describe the separate browsing practices of the Clear Studio website.

Your agenda stays under your control

Tasks, notes, handwriting, habits and Trash are stored locally on your device. Daylight does not send your agenda to a Clear Studio server. App preferences, such as your language and colour palette, are stored on the device.

Optional iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud in Daylight, the app stores and syncs your agenda through iCloud Drive using your Apple Account. The synced archive includes tasks, notes, handwriting, habits and Trash. Appearance preferences remain on the device. You can use Daylight without enabling iCloud. Turning sync off keeps both your local copy and the existing iCloud copy; it does not delete either. Apple operates iCloud under its privacy policy and service terms. Clear Studio does not have access to your private iCloud agenda.

Calendar permission

With your permission, Daylight accesses your calendars to display, create and edit events, and to create notes linked to events. Those notes may contain the event title, date and participants. Events remain managed by their calendar account; they are not synced as events in Daylight’s agenda archive. You can revoke Calendar access in iPad Settings and continue using tasks and notes.

No advertising or tracking SDKs

Daylight does not include advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. Apple may process App Store, TestFlight and diagnostic information under its own policies and your device settings. If you send feedback or a crash report through Apple, information made available to the developer may be used to investigate problems and improve the app.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot issues, on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing support. Our email service provider processes these messages to deliver the service. We retain correspondence only as long as needed for the request and any applicable legal obligations. Please avoid sending private notes or a complete agenda backup unless needed and agreed.

Retention, deletion and copies

You control the content you keep in the app. Deleted tasks, notes and habits remain in Trash for 30 days unless permanently deleted sooner. Expired items are removed when the app next performs cleanup. Sync recovery copies are stored separately on the device and do not follow the Trash’s 30-day rule. Exported backups remain wherever you saved them until you delete them. Deleting content from the active agenda does not erase historical recovery copies or exported backups. To remove all copies, review local app data, exported files, device backups and Daylight’s iCloud storage. Disable Daylight sync on your devices before removing its iCloud data to avoid uploading a local copy again.

Your choices and rights

You can export your agenda, edit or delete content, disable sync, and revoke Calendar access. For personal information you have sent to Clear Studio, you can contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability where applicable, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may also complain to your data protection authority, including Spain’s AEPD. We cannot retrieve or erase your private iCloud agenda on your behalf.

Policy updates

We will update this page if Daylight’s data practices change. Last updated: 5 November 2026.
