A planner. A notebook. Your space.

Structure for your plans.
Freedom for your thoughts.

Some things belong on a calendar. Others start as a scribble, a list or a thought in the margin.

Daylight brings them closer. See what’s ahead, write down what matters, and keep the details nearby — without turning every thought into a form.

01

Your day, in context.

See events from your Apple calendars alongside your tasks and a space for notes in the margin. Your commitments and your thinking, on the same page.

02

Your week. Your month.

Switch between weekly tasks and a seven-day agenda. Step back to a monthly calendar, with all-day events first and more events just a tap away. Choose which calendars to see in every view.

03

Write the way you think.

Write directly in task rows, in the margins, on your weekly agenda and inside monthly day boxes. Switch to the keyboard for typed tasks and notes. Your existing ink stays visible.

04

Plans change. Move with them.

Move events to another time or day and adjust their duration with the handles. Open an event to edit its title and details. Changes save back to its original Apple calendar; read-only calendars stay protected.

05

A notebook that stays close.

Give meetings and ideas their own pages. Add titles, participants, typed notes and drawings. Search your typed notes, or hide the index when you need more space.

06

Small habits. A wider view.

Keep track of the routines you want to make room for, with a monthly habit tracker. And capture unscheduled tasks in your inbox until you’re ready to place them.

From the first thought to the next step

Keep the thought.
Find the next step.

A simple rhythm for the things you need to do — and everything that sits behind them.

01 / CAPTURE

Just write it down.

Add a task directly in your day or week, with a keyboard or Apple Pencil. No detail window to interrupt the thought.

02 / DEVELOP

Open up the details.

Tap an existing task to open its own space. Keep supporting notes and drawings inside, while the list stays compact.

03 / CONTINUE

Carry it forward.

Reorder your tasks or move them to another day. Keep a quick overview outside and the fuller picture within reach.

A few things to know

Before the
first page.

When will Daylight be available?

Daylight is being tested and refined through everyday use. A release date hasn’t been announced yet. It will be available through the Apple App Store when it’s ready.

Do I need an Apple Pencil?

No. You can use a keyboard for tasks and typed notes. Apple Pencil adds freehand writing and drawing when you want a more personal, open-ended way to work.

Does it work with my existing calendars?

Daylight connects to calendars available through Apple Calendar, with your permission. You can bring your existing events into your day rather than recreate them in a separate planner.

Will it sync with iCloud?

Optional automatic iCloud sync is planned for release. It’s still in preparation. The current version saves locally and supports exporting a backup.

Which devices will it support?

Daylight is being designed for iPad. Supported iPad models and the minimum iPadOS version will be confirmed before launch. iPhone and Mac versions haven’t been announced.

Is there a subscription?

The proposed launch model is a one-time purchase of €14.99, without a recurring subscription. Pricing is provisional until the App Store release is confirmed.
